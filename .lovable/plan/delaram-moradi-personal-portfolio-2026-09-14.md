# Delaram Moradi — Personal Portfolio

## Direction

Create a polished, immersive portfolio with a near-black cosmic background, deep purple and blue accents, moving stars, and a subtle luminous sparkle that follows pointer movement. Keep the interface elegant and restrained so the effects frame the work rather than distract from it.

## Pages

- **Home:** Delaram Moradi as the main first-screen statement, a concise portfolio introduction, and clear paths into Projects and Research & Teaching.
- **Projects:** A strong portfolio structure with an intentional empty state, ready for future project imagery, descriptions, roles, and links.
- **Research & Teaching:** Separate, clearly organized areas for future publications, research interests, courses, and teaching material without inventing entries.
- **Contact:** A minimal social-links presentation with clearly marked placeholders for Delaram’s real profiles.

## Shared Experience

- Add a consistent navigation and compact footer across all pages.
- Build layered animated decorative stars with varied depth and speed.
- Add a refined cursor-following glow and short-lived spark particles on pointer movement.
- Provide touch-friendly behavior and respect reduced-motion settings.
- Use responsive layouts with strong typography, generous space, accessible contrast, keyboard focus states, and readable mobile navigation.

## Visual System

- Use semantic design tokens for near-black space, deep violet, midnight blue, luminous white, and restrained cool highlights.
- Favor crisp type, subtle glass-like surfaces, thin borders, and limited glow rather than oversized effects or generic gradients.
- Use motion selectively: slow environmental movement, smooth page feedback, and no constant animation on content.

## Content and Metadata

- Keep all portfolio, research, teaching, and social content as editable structure or explicit placeholders.
- Add unique titles and descriptions for every page, with Delaram Moradi’s name and portfolio purpose represented consistently.

## Validation

- Check every page on desktop and mobile for readability, clean spacing, navigation, effects, and motion accessibility.
- Confirm pointer sparkles do not block clicks or reduce performance.
- Verify all routes load cleanly with no visible errors.

## Scope Note

This version is presentation-only: no contact form, stored submissions, or other connected services are included.
